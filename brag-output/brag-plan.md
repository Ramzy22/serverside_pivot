# Brag Plan: serverside-pivot

## 1. Angle & Hook
* **The Angle:** The Uncrashable Pivot Engine. Perspective crashes your browser with WebAssembly OOM. AG Grid costs $2,500 and gives you an empty backend. `serverside-pivot` connects to any database in 2 lines of Python and gives you a turnkey, virtualized pivot table with charts, sparklines, and zero RAM explosion.
* **The 3-Step Flow:**
  1. Low-code: 2 lines of Python.
  2. Any data source: 10 KB file to 100M+ row warehouse (DuckDB, Snowflake, Postgres).
  3. The Web UI: 50,000 rows scrolling in 12ms with inline sparkline cells, docked charts, and heatmaps.
